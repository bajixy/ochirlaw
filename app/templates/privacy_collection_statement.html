Privacy Collection Statement

Ochir Law Pty Ltd ACN 692 540 426 (“Ochir Law”, “we”, “us” and “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Collection Statement should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. Your personal information

Personal information collected, held and used by Ochir Law may include your name, address, date of birth, phone number, email address, gender and other categories of personal information detailed in our Privacy Policy.

2. How we collect personal information

Ochir Law collects personal information in several ways, including when:

  • We provide services via email or our website;
  • We provide assistance or support for our products or services;
  • You participate in functions, events, activities or social media pages;
  • You request information about our products or services;
  • You upload or submit information to us or our website; or
  • You complete forms, surveys or provide feedback to us.

3. Why we collect, use and disclose personal information

Ochir Law collects, stores, uses and discloses personal information for operational and marketing purposes, including to:

  • Contact you about Ochir Law’s products and services or an ongoing matter;
  • Provide marketing material and information about products, services and events;
  • Send documents or other materials;
  • Process payments;
  • Research and develop Ochir Law’s services;
  • Analyse use, activity and performance of our website, products and services; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

4. Direct marketing

We may use personal information for direct marketing. You may opt out using the unsubscribe link in marketing material or by contacting us.

5. What happens if you do not provide personal information

You do not have to provide us with your personal information. Where possible, we will give you the option to interact with us anonymously or by using a pseudonym. However, if you choose not to provide information we require, we may not be able to effectively interact with you or provide services to you.

6. Who we disclose personal information to

Ochir Law may disclose personal information to:

  • Third parties connected with the purposes listed above;
  • Our professional advisers;
  • A buyer or successor in interest of our business and their advisers; and
  • Other parties where required or permitted by law.

Some third parties may be based in or have servers located outside Australia. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to maintain data security and appropriate privacy practices.

7. Accuracy, access and complaints

We take reasonable steps to ensure personal information is accurate, complete and up to date. Our Privacy Policy explains how you may access or correct personal information, or make a privacy complaint.

8. Contact details

If you have questions or concerns about privacy and your personal information, contact Ochir Law at m.saruul@ochirlaw.com.au or call 0456 867 879.
